# DRAG

Paste a link, click **GRAB**, and the video or audio lands in your edit. Works with YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and most other sites, straight into Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or any editor by dragging.

## Install

1. Download the DMG for your Mac from the [latest release](https://github.com/karimkhater/drag/releases/latest):
   - **Apple-Silicon** for M1/M2/M3/M4 Macs
   - **Intel** for older Intel Macs
   
   Not sure which you have? Apple menu → About This Mac → look at "Chip".
2. Open the DMG and drag **DRAG** into **Applications**.
3. Open DRAG. The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the developer. Click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.

## Updates

DRAG updates itself. When a new version is ready you'll see a **Restart to update** button in the app.

## Use

- Click the paste button (or just copy a link and click back into DRAG).
- Pick **Video** or **Audio** and the quality, then click **GRAB**.
- When it's done, drag the card straight onto your timeline. With Final Cut Pro open, clips are also imported into an event called **DRAG**.
- Files are saved to `~/Movies/DRAG` (change it with **Browse…**).

Only download content you have the rights to use.
